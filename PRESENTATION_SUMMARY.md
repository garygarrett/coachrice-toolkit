# CoachRICE Coaching Education & Training Platform
## Stakeholder & Participant Presentation Summary

---

## Executive Overview

CoachRICE is a comprehensive digital coaching education and training platform designed to enhance coach development through evidence-based assessment tools, practice scenarios, and performance tracking. The platform combines multiple learning modalities to accelerate competency development aligned with the 2025 BARS (Behavioral Assessment Rating Scale) coaching standards.

---

## Core Value Proposition

### For Coaches & Participants
- **Accelerated Competency Development**: Practice coaching skills in structured, low-risk environments before applying them with real clients
- **Evidence-Based Feedback**: Immediate assessment of coaching performance using validated BARS competency framework
- **Flexible Learning**: Self-paced tools accessible anytime, enabling continuous skill refinement
- **Measurable Progress**: Track improvement over time with detailed performance metrics and history

### For Program Leaders & Administrators
- **Standardized Assessment**: Consistent evaluation across all participants using 2025 BARS standards
- **Comprehensive Visibility**: Real-time dashboards showing participant progress, completion rates, and competency development
- **Scalable Training**: Support multiple coaches simultaneously without proportional resource increases
- **Data-Driven Insights**: Identify skill gaps, training effectiveness, and areas requiring additional support

## 2025 BARS Competency Assessment

The platform implements the November 2025 BARS (Behavioral Assessment Rating Scale) framework:

### Rating Scale
- **Exceeds the Standard (5)**: Demonstrates advanced, exemplary coaching behavior
- **Meets the Standard (4)**: Demonstrates proficient coaching behavior
- **Below the Standard (2)**: Demonstrates developing coaching behavior
- **Does Not Meet Standard (1)**: Significant gaps in coaching behavior
- **N/A**: Not applicable to scenario; excluded from competency averages

### 20 Behavioral Statements (A3.1 - A8.3)
Covers coaching competencies including:
- Establishing effective relationships
- Active listening and reflection
- Goal setting and planning
- Feedback delivery
- Problem-solving and decision-making approaches
- Professional development planning
- Special binary assessment (A8.1) on specific high-impact coaching skill

### Scoring & Reporting
- Immediate quantitative feedback using formula: (Raw Score − 1) × 2.5
- Competency-level aggregation for holistic development view
- Comparable across multiple assessments to track growth

**Value**: Standardized, validated framework ensures consistency and credibility
